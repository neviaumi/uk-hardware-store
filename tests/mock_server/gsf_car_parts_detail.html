How It Works

To qualify, the product must be identical to the one purchased (same brand, model/part number, full specification, and sold as new with a UK manufacturer warranty). The competitor must be a UK‑VAT Registered Retailer, with the item in stock and able to ship immediately to your postcode.

We compare the competitor’s lowest publicly available price, including VAT and the cheapest available delivery option. Any codes or offers must be visible on the competitor’s website. We cannot accept:

  • Sign‑up or new customer discount codes

  • Exclusive promotional/voucher codes sent via email or SMS

  • App‑only, loyalty, membership or personalised pricing

Marketplaces (eg. eBay, Amazon, AutoDoc), second‑hand items are not included in the price match.
